# GKK Intern

**Less watching. More building.**

Turn what you know into work you can show. GKK Intern is a remote internship program where you build practical skills through engineering projects, mentorship and thoughtful review. By Global Kompass Krew, a Bubblesort venture. Code. Build. Deploy.

## What you walk away with
- **Stipend:** up to ₹8,000 a month on eligible internships, for interns who consistently ship quality work
- **Letter of Recommendation:** a personal LOR from your mentor about what you built
- **Verified certificate:** issued with a unique ID and QR code that anyone can check online
- **Placement support:** resume and interview preparation, and referrals to hiring partners

## Open internships
- AI & Machine Learning Engineering Internship
- Full-Stack Web & Cloud Internship
- Mobile App Engineering Internship

3 months, 100% remote, cohort-based.

## Apply
https://gkkintern.in

Stipend, LOR and placement support are earned through performance and vary by internship. We do not promise a job, and we will never ask you to pay for a stipend or a recommendation.
